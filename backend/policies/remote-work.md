# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation may work remotely, subject to their manager's approval. Roles that require on-site presence, such as office administration, are not eligible.

## Remote days per week
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Remote days must be agreed with your manager in advance and recorded in the team calendar. Wednesday is a team day when everyone works from the office.

## Equipment and internet allowance
Acme provides a laptop and headset for remote work. Employees approved for remote work receive an internet allowance of BDT 1,500 per month, paid with salary.

## Security when working remotely
Always connect through the company VPN. Do not work on confidential data from public Wi-Fi, and lock your screen whenever you step away. Report a lost or stolen device to IT at it@acme.example within 1 hour.
