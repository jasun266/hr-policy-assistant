# Expenses and Travel Policy

## Claiming expenses
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense with a receipt for every item. Claims without receipts are only accepted for amounts under BDT 500. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next salary payment.

## Business travel
Business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Hotel bookings should stay within BDT 8,000 per night in Dhaka and BDT 6,000 per night elsewhere in Bangladesh.

## Daily allowance
When travelling for work outside your home city you receive a daily allowance of BDT 1,200 for meals. The allowance is not paid for days when meals are provided by the event or client.

## Training and certifications
Each employee has a learning budget of BDT 40,000 per year for courses, books and certification exams related to their role. Get manager approval before spending it; if you leave within 6 months of a certification paid by Acme, its cost may be recovered.
