# Leave Policy

## Leave entitlement
Every full-time employee receives the following paid leave each calendar year (January to December):
- Annual leave: 20 working days
- Sick leave: 14 working days
- Casual leave: 10 working days
Entitlement is pro-rated for employees who join during the year.

## Annual leave
Annual leave is for planned time off such as holidays and personal travel. Apply at least 7 days in advance for requests of 3 or more working days. Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
Sick leave covers your own illness or medical appointments. Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## Casual leave
Casual leave is for short, unplanned personal matters. A single casual leave request may not exceed 3 consecutive working days. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave in the same request and does not carry forward.

## Working days, weekend and public holidays
The Dhaka office works Sunday to Thursday; Friday and Saturday are the weekend. Leave is counted in working days only, so weekends and public holidays inside a request are not deducted. Public holidays observed by the Dhaka office include 21 February, 26 March, 14 April, 1 May, 16 December and 25 December, plus other government-announced holidays.

## How to apply for leave
Leave requests are created in the HR portal. The HR assistant can draft a request for you, but you must review and confirm the draft yourself before it is sent to your manager. A request cannot span two calendar years; split it into two requests instead.

## Approval
Your line manager approves or rejects confirmed requests, normally within 2 working days. Days are reserved from your balance as soon as you confirm a request and are released again if it is rejected or cancelled.

## Cancelling leave
You can cancel a draft or a pending request at any time from the portal. To cancel leave that has already been approved, contact your manager and HR at hr@acme.example.
